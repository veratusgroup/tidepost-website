Privacy policy

How Tidepost handles game data, purchases and support messages.

Draft for review — not yet effective. The purchase SDK data flows and support-service details must be verified before this policy is published or used in App Store Connect.

Draft updated 1 October 2026

Who we are

Tidepost is made by Veratus Games, a brand of Veratus Group Limited. Contact us about this policy at sayhello@veratusgames.com.

This notice covers the Tidepost iPhone and iPad app, these support pages and messages you send us.

Your game progress

The game stores stage progress, collected letters, the route in progress and preferences on your device. Tidepost does not require a game account or provide cloud progress sync. Removing the app can remove this local data. Device backups are controlled by your operating system and account settings.

Purchases

Apple processes App Store payments. The game uses purchase and entitlement information to determine whether the full game is unlocked and to restore that access. We do not receive your full payment card details through Tidepost.

The current app integrates Unity In-App Purchasing 5.4.3. Unity documents identifier, device, session and country data processing for IAP 5.4 and later. The exact data sent by Tidepost’s configuration is still being verified; this draft does not claim that the app collects no data.

See Apple’s privacy information and Unity’s IAP privacy information.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your question and investigate the issue. You do not need to include sensitive information, passwords or payment card details.

Support correspondence is handled through our email service. Before publication, we will confirm the service provider, processing locations, lawful basis and retention policy in this notice.

This website

These pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms or embedded third-party media. They use local styles and system fonts.

If hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes technical request information needed to serve and protect the site, such as an IP address. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following an external link or sending email involves the relevant provider’s services.

Retention and your choices

Local game data remains on your device until removed by you or the operating system. Store transaction records are managed by Apple under its policies. The final notice will state how long support and SDK-related records are retained and explain any relevant international transfer safeguards.

Depending on the law that applies, you may ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold, or request restriction, portability or object to its use. Email sayhello@veratusgames.com. We may need to verify your identity to respond.

You can raise a privacy concern with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office or your local data protection authority.

Children

The game does not require children to provide a name or create an account. A parent or guardian can contact us about information a child may have sent to support. The final age rating and SDK practices must be reviewed before launch.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and show the effective date of the published policy.
